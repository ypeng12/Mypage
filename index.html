Peer-Reviewed Journal

International Cargo Charter Operations and Preighter Performances in the Context of COVID-19: A Case of China

Chuntao Wu, Xiaohe He, Yuliang Peng
Transport Policy · Vol. 184, Article 104173 · 2026
DOI: 10.1016/j.tranpol.2026.104173
Article Read PDF

Abstract: This research presents the first comprehensive empirical study of international air cargo charter networks and passenger-to-cargo ("preighter") operations under external system shocks. Leveraging a proprietary dataset of CAAC flight permits, we constructed undirected networks at the city level, representing over 80,000 annual flights.

Network Science Methodologies: We formulated the charter logistics system as an undirected graph and computed topological indicators across time-varying snapshots:

  • Average Degree & Path Length: Quantified the densification of hub-and-spoke models, proving structural stability and small-world properties.
  • Node & Edge Betweenness Centrality: Isolated critical routing bridges and tracked dynamic adjustments under intense route volatility.
  • Operational Flexibility: Identified the pivotal structural role of freight forwarders (charterers) who integrated truck-to-air transit to maintain supply-chain robustness.
